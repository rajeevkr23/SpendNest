Privacy Policy for SpendNest – Expense Tracker
Effective Date: 8 September 2026
SpendNest ("we", "our", or "the app") is an offline expense tracking application designed to help users manage their personal finances, track expenses, and create budgets.
Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use SpendNest.
1. Information We Collect
SpendNest is designed to work primarily offline.
We do not require users to create an account, provide their name, email address, phone number, or any personal identification information.
The app may store the following information locally on your device:
* Expense records
* Budget information
* Categories
* Notes added by the user
* Receipt images added by the user
* App preferences and settings
This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.
2. Data Storage
SpendNest uses local device storage to save your expense data.
Your financial information remains on your device unless you choose to export, share, or back up your data using features provided within the app.
We do not have access to your personal expense records.
3. Camera and Storage Permissions
If you use receipt scanning or receipt attachment features, SpendNest may request permission to access:
* Camera (to capture receipt images)
* Photos/storage (to attach or save receipt images)
These permissions are only used for features requested by the user.
4. Advertising
SpendNest may use third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob to display advertisements.
Ad providers may collect certain information, such as:
* Device information
* Advertising identifiers
* App interaction information
This information is used to provide and improve advertising services.
You can learn more about Google's privacy practices here:
https://policies.google.com/privacy
5. In-App Purchases
SpendNest may offer optional premium features through in-app purchases.
Payments are processed securely by Google Play. We do not collect or store your payment information.
6. Data Security
We take reasonable measures to protect the app and your data.
Because SpendNest stores financial information locally on your device, you are responsible for protecting your device with appropriate security measures such as a password, PIN, or biometric lock.
7. Children's Privacy
SpendNest is not directed toward children under the age of 13.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
8. Third-Party Services
SpendNest may use the following third-party services:
* Google AdMob (advertising)
* Google Play Billing (payments)
These services have their own privacy policies governing their data practices.
9. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
